# J1 preregistered pilot
Can a trained small policy compose sequential repair operations on real benign RGB images under a fixed operation budget? Controlled Pillow primitives; independent exact RGB bytes plus mode/size oracle checks completed output. Source and exact target are generated, target exposed at up to nine fixed sampled pixels per live state (all methods get same nine target RGB values); complete target is private to fixture/oracle. Shared published primitives: seven Pillow D4 transposes, invert, RGB cyclic shifts either direction. All methods use actual Pillow jobs to emit and execute at most 3 primitives and may stop earlier; deriving a label, feature, or lookup on nine RGB samples is not counted as a full primitive execution. At execution time all methods receive the source image, use up to nine samples for each candidate geometry or color mapping from source per state, and no full-image trial/lookahead exempt from the action cap.
Training with 800 fixtures, 32 fixed possible generated algebra combinations of one or two nonidentity components (geometry / invert / RGB cycle), sampled across source patterns. Training labels are independently constructed known inverses of injected factors in canonical geometry→invert→cycle order, dropping individually ineffective steps on exact oracle (training only). Test uses disjoint image seeds, source families random-color (separate combinations), impulse-on-gray background (distribution shift), gray-horizontal bilateral symmetry (distribution shift). Per family test 320 depth-three fixtures with 7×1×2 possible combinations; thus test combos are disjoint, and outer-family shifts are disjoint from all training images. In test do not train/update and do not reveal pass/fail until a full path ends. Production users asking for robot/physical actions out of scope.
Baselines: native direct factored Pillow transforms using handcrafted diagnostic anchor matching and same nine target pixels, sparse one-step greedy search in a weakly factor-blind RGB match metric, nearest-neighbor retrieval of a labeled state, and same model with fully shuffled training labels. Candidate is ExtraTrees on same per-state geometry/color matching summaries used by retrieval; stop is its own class. Native baseline can exactly factor symmetry and color evidence when the anchors are informative. Evaluate success per family and paired advantages, primitive count, stage selections. Admission requires >=5pp improvement over strongest non-shuffled comparator in at least two of three held-out groups and no >5pp loss anywhere. Artifact correctness, not model teacher capabilities. Exploratory design no claims of prior art novelty or actual production improvement.
